Case Study

ProspectPulse — AI Prospect Research in Under 60 Seconds

An AI tool that finds the right decision-maker, researches them, and scores the fit — turning up to two hours of manual prospecting into one minute.

The Problem

Across ten years in B2B sales and nonprofit fundraising, the same task ate my time every single week: researching a prospect before reaching out.

Finding the right decision-maker at a target company

~20 minutes, often more when there was no prior relationship.

Building a complete brief

Company background, the contact's likely priorities, timing signals, and an honest read on whether they were even worth pursuing: 90 minutes to 2 hours per prospect.

Multiply that across a full pipeline and it's days of work that never directly closes anything. For a small sales team or a nonprofit development office with no dedicated research function, that time simply doesn't exist — so the research gets skipped, and outreach goes out cold.

What I Built

ProspectPulse is a seller-aware AI research tool. You give it three things: a target company, the decision-maker role you need to reach, and what you or your organization does (plus an optional ideal-partner profile). It runs live web research and returns a complete brief in under 60 seconds.

SectionWhat It Delivers
Company briefWhat they do, size, and the most relevant recent news
Identified contactThe actual named person who holds (or best matches) the role you asked for
Contact briefTheir likely priorities and public activity
Buying signalsTiming indicators — giving cycles, initiatives, recent announcements
Fit scorecardFive dimensions scored 1–5 against a fixed rubric, each with its evidence
Value prop mapTheir situation mapped to your angle — to frame the conversation, not as outreach copy

The Result

I tested ProspectPulse on three major Omaha corporations, using a youth-mentoring nonprofit seeking a corporate sponsorship as the seller. The tool didn't just return data — it returned calibrated, honest judgment.

CompanyFitWhat the Tool Surfaced
Union Pacific 4.4 — Strong Found Liisa Lawson Stark (VP Public Affairs) and flagged that the 2026 Community Ties grant cycle is open right now — timing scored 5/5. Correctly identified UP as the best current target.
First National Bank of Omaha 3.8 — Moderate No literal "VP of Community Relations" exists at FNBO — so the tool found the real philanthropy owner, Christina Kahler (Managing Director, Marketing & Philanthropy), instead of forcing a false match.
Kiewit Corporation 3.6 — Moderate Named Bob Kula (VP Corporate Communication) as the closest contact and flagged honestly that he isn't the Community Relations owner and the exact owner is unclear — rather than inflating the match.

That variation is the point. A research tool that rates every prospect "strong" is useless. ProspectPulse gave a defensible reason for every score and told me plainly that Union Pacific — with an open grant cycle — was the prospect to prioritize this quarter. Each brief surfaced a real named contact, specific dollar figures, and active giving cycles, and was honest about where the fit was imperfect.
