The work isn't new. The tools are.

My background is in sales, business development, and nonprofit fundraising — but much of the work underneath those titles has always been operational: managing pipelines, redesigning CRM workflows, coordinating stakeholders, and turning inconsistent work into repeatable systems. Career Snapshot

Through 2025 — Sales, Business Development & Nonprofit Fundraising

Eleven years across radio, SaaS (Hudl, Toast), sports media (Mountain West Conference, Learfield), ed-tech (Teacher Created Materials), and nonprofit development (TeamMates Mentoring). My formal roles centered on revenue and relationships, but the work consistently required building systems behind them: managing complex pipelines, improving CRM usage, analyzing performance, coordinating stakeholders, creating repeatable processes, and solving operational problems that affected execution.

2025–Present — Operations, Systems & Automation

Began focusing more deliberately on the systems underneath the work: how organizations move information, prioritize opportunities, coordinate people, and execute repeatable processes. That work increasingly incorporates AI and automation — not as the objective, but as tools for improving the operation.

2026 — Mission Capacity

Founded Mission Capacity to help Omaha nonprofits and mission-driven organizations improve the workflows and systems behind their work. Engagements begin by understanding the existing operation, identifying the highest-value bottleneck, and determining the simplest useful intervention — whether that's a redesigned process, better information flow, automation, or an AI-enabled tool.

The goal isn't to introduce more technology. It's to leave the organization operating better than it did before.

Also Open To

Alongside Mission Capacity, I'm open to full-time opportunities in business operations, process improvement, business systems, implementation, project delivery, and AI-enabled transformation — particularly roles where I can help an organization understand how work gets done, improve the systems behind it, and turn better ideas into repeatable execution.
