Case Study · Heartland Family Service

Fixing a Broken Marketing Request Workflow Without Buying New Software

A marketing team's Trello board had stopped showing where work stood. We cut the workflow to nine stages with a named sign-off at each, redesigned the request form, and made form requests become Trello cards automatically. $0 in new tools.

Case Study · Jet Award Foundation

A CRM Inside Gmail and Google Sheets

Sponsor, donor, and ticket data was scattered across Zeffy, Square, event platforms, checks, and emailed lists. I built a CRM around the Gmail and Google Sheets the director already uses: one label on an email, and the dashboard updates. No new login, no new subscription.
