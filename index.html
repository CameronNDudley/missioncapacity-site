Current Engagement

Jet Award Foundation · Discovery completed August 2026 · In progress

What the audit actually found

The Jet Award Foundation is a one-person nonprofit in Omaha that runs a national college football award and funds scholarships. I sat down with its Executive Director for a 90-minute workflow audit.

The first thing it surfaced was that a tool I had already built solved a problem they didn't have. Their weekly content process took about an hour, and he enjoyed doing it. The real cost sat somewhere else entirely — grant research and corporate sponsorship prospecting, including a grant platform they'd just cancelled as too expensive.

So I told him that, and changed the plan. That's what the audit is for. The finding that saves an organization the most money is often "don't build that."

In progress: tuning ProspectPulse to the foundation's corporate sponsorship pipeline.

Why Mission Capacity Exists

Eleven years in sales, business development, and nonprofit fundraising taught me exactly where revenue and operations break — because I ran them. Over the past year I've put that experience to work rebuilding the systems behind how organizations operate, using whatever actually fixes the problem: a redesigned process, better information flow, automation, or AI.

Meanwhile, the tools available to fix these problems have never been more accessible — and the organizations that actually serve this city are often the least equipped to evaluate or use them.

That's the gap I work in. Mission Capacity runs hands-on operational audits that map an organization's real workflows — fundraising, communications, program reporting — then builds and implements whatever removes the biggest bottleneck.

The goal isn't to hand organizations more technology. It's to leave their staff able to run a better process without me.

How an Engagement Works

1 · Audit I map how your team actually works — not the org chart, the real workflow. Where hours disappear, where handoffs break, where staff are doing work no one should be doing by hand.
2 · Build I build and implement the specific tool that removes the biggest bottleneck. Not a platform subscription. A working thing, scoped to one real problem.
3 · Handoff I train your staff to run it without me, and document it so it survives turnover. An engagement is finished when you don't need me.

Behind the work: eleven years managing revenue, pipelines, and complex B2B relationships — including nonprofit fundraising from the inside. I know what these workflows cost because I've run them.
